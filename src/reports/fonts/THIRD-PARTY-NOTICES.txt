Third-party notices
==================

This distribution includes subsets of the Noto fonts.

Noto Sans, Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans TC, Noto Sans HK, Noto Sans JP and
Noto Sans KR
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The licence and copyright notice for Noto Sans are in
noto-sans-OFL-1.1.txt. The five CJK families share the licence, copyright
notice and Reserved Font Name declaration in noto-sans-cjk-OFL-1.1.txt.

The font files in this distribution are modified subsets. Each upstream
variable font was pinned to one weight and cut down to the characters that
language's report can draw.

Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/{{COMMIT}}/ofl

Shipped subsets:

  NotoSansSC-zh.ttf (Noto Sans SC) - zh
  NotoSansTC-zh-TW.ttf (Noto Sans TC) - zh-TW
  NotoSansHK-yue.ttf (Noto Sans HK) - yue
  NotoSansJP-ja.ttf (Noto Sans JP) - ja
  NotoSansKR-ko.ttf (Noto Sans KR) - ko
