## English

- **Decimal entry.** In dose and lab fields, `1,5` and `1.5` both save as `1.5`. An ambiguous entry such as `1,234` in English shows a format hint before you can save.
- **Easier logging on small screens.** Headings and bottom tabs wrap at larger text sizes. In History and Labs, the Add, Save, and Cancel buttons have larger touch targets and move to a new row when space runs out.
- **German.** Choose **Deutsch** in **Settings → General → Language**. Dates and numbers use German formatting, and complete backups remember your language choice.
- **Add dose from your home screen.** After initial setup, long-press the app icon and choose **Add dose**. The shortcut keeps any open dose draft. Tap **Save** to record the dose. The shortcut requires Android 7.1 or newer and a compatible launcher.

In What-if, confirming a start time you already set now keeps the original time.

The app runs on Android 7.0 or newer. To update from 1.3.0, open the signed APK and choose **Update**. Your records carry over. [Download the Android APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.4.0/hrt-tracker-1.4.0.apk)

## 简体中文

- **小数输入。** 记用药或填化验结果时，输入 `1,5` 或 `1.5` 都会记成 `1.5`。如果写法有歧义，比如英文界面里的 `1,234`，会提示你改一下再保存。
- **小屏和大字号。** 字号调大后，标题和底部标签会换行。「记录」和「检查」里的添加、保存和取消按钮更容易点中，空间不够时会移到下一行。
- **德语界面。** 在「设置 → 常规设置 → 语言设置」里选「Deutsch」，日期和数字就会按德语习惯显示。完整备份也会记住你的语言选择。
- **桌面快捷方式。** 首次设置完成后，长按应用图标，点「新增用药」就能打开表单。之前没填完的可以接着填，点「保存」才会记下来。这个功能需要 Android 7.1 或更新版本，手机桌面也要支持应用快捷方式。

在「方案试算」里再次确认已经设好的开始时间，现在会保留你原来选的时间。

应用支持 Android 7.0 及以上版本。从 1.3.0 升级时，打开安装包，点「更新」就行，之前的记录都会保留。[下载安卓安装包](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.4.0/hrt-tracker-1.4.0.apk)
