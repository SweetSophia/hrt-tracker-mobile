## English

HRT Tracker 1.0.1 fixes a mode switch that could make your history look empty. In 1.0.0, if Android stopped the app just after you switched modes, it could reopen the previous mode’s history. The app now saves the new mode before showing its checkmark.

To update from 1.0.0, open the signed APK and choose **Update**. Your saved mode, doses, and lab results carry over. [Download the Android APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.0.1/hrt-tracker-1.0.1.apk)
