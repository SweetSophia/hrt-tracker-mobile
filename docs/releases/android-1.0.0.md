## English

HRT Tracker 1.0.0 is the first Android release of the mobile port, maintained by SweetSophia. It brings Oyama’s HRT Tracker to your phone. After installation you can use it offline from the first launch, without an account, a home server, or Tailscale. [Original web project](https://github.com/xunxunProjects/Oyama-s-HRT-Tracker)

- Log doses and lab results, revisit your history, and compare the estimated hormone levels on the chart with your own lab measurements.
- The app stores your dose and lab records on your phone. In Settings you can export or restore a complete backup for both tracking modes. The exported file contains readable health records, so keep it somewhere private.

**Install on Android 7.0 or later.** Download and open `hrt-tracker-1.0.0.apk`. If Android asks, allow installation from the browser or file manager you used. If you already use the signed **HRT Tracker** beta, choose **Update** to keep your records. If the old app is called **HRT Tracker Offline Beta**, export a complete backup from that app and restore it in **HRT Tracker**. [Download the signed APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.0.0/hrt-tracker-1.0.0.apk)

We’d love to hear how it works on your phone. If something goes wrong, tell us your phone model, Android version, and what you did before it happened. Please use sample records in screenshots. [Send feedback](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/issues/new/choose)

## 繁體中文

HRT Tracker Android 版 1.0.0 正式推出了。這款 App 移植自 Oyama 的 HRT Tracker 網頁版，由 SweetSophia 維護。安裝後第一次開啟就能離線使用，不必註冊帳號、自架伺服器或設定 Tailscale。[原版網頁專案](https://github.com/xunxunProjects/Oyama-s-HRT-Tracker)

- 記錄用藥劑量與檢驗結果，隨時回顧過去的紀錄，也能在圖表上對照荷爾蒙濃度的估算曲線與實際檢驗值。
- 用藥紀錄和檢驗結果會儲存在你的手機上。在「設定」裡可以匯出或還原完整備份，兩種追蹤模式的資料都會包含在內。匯出的備份檔內含可直接讀取的健康紀錄，請妥善保管。

**安裝方式（適用於 Android 7.0 以上版本）**

下載並開啟 `hrt-tracker-1.0.0.apk`。若 Android 跳出安裝權限提示，請允許目前使用的瀏覽器或檔案管理工具安裝 App。

之前安裝的是我們提供的 **HRT Tracker** 測試版的話，選「更新」就能保留原有紀錄。舊版名稱若是 **HRT Tracker Offline Beta**，請先在舊版匯出完整備份，再到 **HRT Tracker** 還原。[下載已簽署的 APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.0.0/hrt-tracker-1.0.0.apk)
