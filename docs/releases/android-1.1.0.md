## English

When you change a patch, you can now log taking the old one off and putting the new one on together. In **History**, turn on **Replace previous patch** for a single entry or **Batch add**. If you have several patches of the same hormone, the app selects the oldest eligible one. Leave the switch off when you want to log an additional patch.

For scheduled changes, turn on the same switch for that patch in **Medication plan**. Tapping **Done** records the change at the time you tap. **Undo** takes back the entries from that tap.

To update from 1.0.0 or 1.0.1, open the signed APK and choose **Update**. Your saved records carry over. [Download the Android APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.1.0/hrt-tracker-1.1.0.apk)

## 简体中文

换贴片时，现在可以把揭下旧贴片和贴上新贴片一起记下来了。在「记录」里打开「更换上一片贴片」就行，单独记一片或用「批量添加」都可以。如果同一种激素的贴片还贴着好几片，应用会选最早贴上的那片。只是想多贴一片的话，让开关保持关闭就好。

平时按计划换贴片的话，也可以在「用药方案」里给这款贴片打开同一个开关。到时候点「贴了」，应用就会按当时的时间记下这次更换。点「撤销」可以撤回刚才的记录。

从 1.0.0 或 1.0.1 升级，下载并打开安装包，直接点「更新」就行，原来的记录都会保留。[下载安卓安装包](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.1.0/hrt-tracker-1.1.0.apk)
