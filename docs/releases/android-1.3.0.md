## English

Choose **Terracotta**, **Plum**, or **Teal** in **Settings → General → Appearance → Color theme**. Each palette works with **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. **Black & white** remembers your palette and restores it when you switch back to color.

The estimated curves in **Overview** and **What-if** follow your palette. With **System** selected, the dose heatmap updates when your phone switches between light and dark. Complete backups include your saved palette.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. To update from 1.2.0, open the signed APK and choose **Update**. Your saved records carry over. [Download the Android APK](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.3.0/hrt-tracker-1.3.0.apk)

## 简体中文

打开「设置 → 常规设置 → 外观设置 → 主题配色」，就能选「陶土色」「梅紫色」或「青绿色」。三种配色都能搭配浅色模式、深色模式或跟随系统。切到黑白模式时，之前选的配色会保留，切回其他模式就会恢复。

「概览」和「方案试算」里的估算曲线会跟着配色变。选了「跟随系统」，手机切换浅色或深色时，用药热力图也会跟着调整。完整备份会一并保存你选好的配色。

支持 Android 7.0 及以上版本。从 1.2.0 升级时，打开签名安装包，点「更新」就行。之前的记录都会保留。[下载安卓安装包](https://github.com/SweetSophia/hrt-tracker-mobile/releases/download/android-v1.3.0/hrt-tracker-1.3.0.apk)
